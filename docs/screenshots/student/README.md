# Student

From a new account to a renewing scholar. Each part uses the demo student at that stage.
[Back to all roles](../README.md)

## Part 1: Build a profile (Bea Navarro, new student)

### 1. Home

A new student sees the term, a calendar of important dates, how complete their profile is and what to fill in next.

![Student home for a new account](01-home-new-student.png)

### 2. Your profile

The profile is what scholarship staff see when the student applies. Each section says what's missing.

![Profile overview at 0% complete](02-profile.png)

### 3. Personal details

Name, birth date, sex, phone, address and barangay, years living in Taguig, voter registration and household
income.

![Personal details form](03-profile-personal.png)

### 4. Family

At least a parent or guardian.

![Family section](04-profile-family.png)

### 5. Education

The current or most recent school, with course, year level and grades.

![Education section](05-profile-education.png)

### 6. Profile photo

A clear, recent photo of the face, JPG or PNG up to 5 MB.

![Profile photo upload](06-profile-photo.png)

### 7. Find scholarships (before the profile is filled in)

Matching needs the profile, so a new student is sent there first.

![Find scholarships asking for a profile first](07-find-scholarships-no-profile.png)

## Part 2: Find a program and apply (Carolyn Hawkins, applicant)

### 8. Home

With a near-complete profile, the home page adds upcoming deadlines and the student's applications.

![Student home for an applicant](08-home-applicant.png)

### 9. Find scholarships

Every program's rules are checked against the profile. Each rule shows as met or not, with the allowance tiers on
offer.

![Find scholarships with matched programs](09-find-scholarships.png)

### 10. Start an application

A last eligibility check before the application is created.

![Apply to TCU CEAA Scholarship](10-start-application.png)

### 11. Your applications

Every application with its term and status (draft, submitted, returned, approved and so on).

![List of the student's applications](11-applications.png)

### 12. Application

School and grades are copied from the profile when the student submits. Each required document gets its own
upload. The application is submitted once all the required documents are in.

![A draft application with document uploads](12-application-detail.png)

### 13. Notifications

Updates about applications, allowances and support replies.

![Student notifications](13-notifications.png)

## Part 3: Fix a returned application (Miguel Bautista)

### 14. A returned application

Staff can send an application back instead of rejecting it.

![Applications list showing a returned application](14-applications-returned.png)

### 15. What to fix

The staff's reason is shown at the top, and each declined document says why, so the student can upload a better
copy and submit again.

![Returned application with the staff's reason and a declined document](15-application-returned-detail.png)

## Part 4: Being a scholar (Carmela Villanueva)

### 16. Home

A scholar's home shows the scholarship card: tier, allowance per term, next release and what it takes to renew.

![Student home for a scholar](16-home-scholar.png)

### 17. Your scholarships

Each term's school record, the allowances received so far and the distribution days still to come.

![Scholarship with terms and payouts](17-scholarships.png)

### 18. Renewal

A renewal is an application for the next term. It checks the renewal rules (GWA and units) and asks for the
renewal documents.

![Renewal application](18-renewal.png)

## Part 5: On hold, and getting help (Elena Garcia)

### 19. Suspended scholarship

A suspended scholarship stays visible with its history, but no payouts are scheduled.

![A suspended scholarship](19-scholarship-on-hold.png)

### 20. Contact the scholarship office

The student picks a topic and, if it's about a program, which one. Program messages go to that program's staff and to
the scholarship office.

![Contact the office form](20-contact-the-office.png)

### 21. Replies

Answers to the student's messages, newest first.

![Support replies](21-support-replies.png)
