# Program staff

Krista Kelley, staff for the TCU CEAA Scholarship. Staff only see the programs they belong to. Heads see these
screens too, plus [their own](../program-head/README.md). [Back to all roles](../README.md)

## 1. Dashboard

Active scholars, applications waiting, money released this year, a calendar of release days and tasks, and what needs
attention.

![Program dashboard](01-dashboard.png)

## Applications

### 2. Application queue

Filter by status, term and kind (new or renewal). Several applications can be acted on at once.

![Applications queue](02-applications-queue.png)

### 3. Review an application

The applicant's details, each document to preview and approve or decline, the eligibility check, and the decision:
approve with a tier, return for changes, or don't approve. The history lists every step.

![Application review](03-application-review.png)

### 4. File for a student

Staff can fill in and submit an application for a student whose profile they made, or a student they find by
account email.

![File for a student](04-apply-on-behalf.png)

## Scholars

### 5. Scholar list

Everyone with an award in this program, by status, with CSV/XLSX export and import.

![Scholar list](05-scholars.png)

### 6. Scholar details

A drawer with the scholar's school, allowance, start term and renewal progress.

![Scholar details drawer](06-scholar-detail.png)

### 7. Add a scholar

Gives one of the program's students an award without an application.

![Add scholar](07-add-scholar.png)

### 8. Import scholars

Adds many scholars at once from the CSV/XLSX template. Nothing is saved unless every row passes.

![Import scholars](08-import-scholars.png)

## Money

### 9. Disbursements

Payouts grouped by distribution day: how many and how much are prepared, approved and released.

![Disbursements overview](09-disbursements.png)

### 10. A distribution day

The budget at a glance and the list of payouts for that day. Staff prepare payouts; a program head approves and
releases them.

![Payouts for one distribution day](10-disbursement-schedule.png)

### 11. Schedules

Distribution days with their term and claim location. Scholars see published schedules.

![Distribution schedules](11-distribution-schedules.png)

### 12. Budget

The year's budget, what's released, what's approved but not yet released, and what's left.

![Budget for the year](12-budget.png)

## Students

### 13. Students

Profiles staff created that the student hasn't claimed yet, with their invitation status.

![Unclaimed student profiles](13-students.png)

### 14. New student profile

Creates a profile and emails the student an invitation to claim it.

![New student profile form](14-create-student-profile.png)

## Reports, support and tasks

### 15. Reports

The program's reports to the scholarship office and where each one is: with the head, with the office, returned or
accepted.

![Program reports](15-reports.png)

### 16. Send a report

A title, period, description and PDF. It goes to the program head first, then to the office.

![Send a report form](16-new-report.png)

### 17. Support inbox

Students' messages about this program, answered in the app or by email.

![Program support inbox](17-support-inbox.png)

### 18. Answer a request

Reply in the app or compose an email. Either can start from a reply template.

![Support request with reply options](18-support-request.png)

### 19. Tasks

Private to-dos with due dates. Overdue ones also show on the dashboard.

![Tasks](19-tasks.png)

### 20. Notifications

For example, a report the head approved or the office accepted.

![Staff notifications](20-notifications.png)
