# Scholarship office

Jessica Tate, the scholarship office (super admin). The office works across every program.
[Back to all roles](../README.md)

## 1. Overview

Totals across programs, a calendar of every program's dates, and what needs the office's attention.

![Office overview](01-overview.png)

## Programs

### 2. Programs

Every program with its application window and status. Programs are data, so a new one needs no code. A new
program stays closed until the office opens it.

![Programs list](02-programs.png)

### 3. Edit a program

Its name and description. Rules, tiers and documents are under the program's **Settings**.

![Edit program](03-program-edit.png)

### 4. New program

A short name for links (it can't change later), a name and a description.

![New program form](04-new-program.png)

## Accounts

### 5. Staff and office accounts

Every staff and office account, their programs and whether they're active. Students sign up themselves, so they
aren't listed.

![Staff and office accounts](05-staff.png)

### 6. Account details

Status, last sign-in and program memberships. Changing memberships asks for the password.

![Account details](06-staff-detail.png)

### 7. New account

The new person gets an email with a link to set their password.

![New account form](07-new-staff.png)

### 8. Scholars across programs

### 9. Schools

### 10. School terms
