About this demo

How do I try each role?

Open the sign-in page, choose "Exploring ScholarSync? Use a test account" and pick a student, program staff, a program head or the scholarship office. Every test account uses the same published password.

Why did my changes disappear?

The demo resets every night around 03:00 Manila time: every change and upload is deleted and the fictional sample data comes back.

Why can't I change the password or email of a test account?

The accounts are shared by every visitor, so their sign-in details, email addresses and two-factor settings are locked. New accounts can't be created either.

Will I get emails?

No. The demo sends no email. Notices and support replies appear in the app instead.
