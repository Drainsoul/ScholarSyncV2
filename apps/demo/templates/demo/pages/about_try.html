{# The hosted demo (branch `demo`): the About page's "Try it" list. #}
  • You are on the public demo: sign in with one of the test accounts listed on the sign-in page to see each role. They share one published password.
  • Its data is fictional and resets every night around 03:00 Manila time, so anything you add is gone the next day. No email is sent.
  • To keep your own copy, run it on your computer with docker compose up. The README explains how.
  • Read the FAQ for the common questions.
