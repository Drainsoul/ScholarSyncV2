{# The hosted demo (branch `demo`): what is different about this copy's data. #}

This copy is a public demo with fictional data and shared test accounts. Please don't enter real personal information or upload real documents.

Everything entered or uploaded here is deleted every night around 03:00 Manila time, when the demo goes back to its sample data. No email is sent: messages the app would send are written to the host's short-lived logs instead.

It runs on Vercel (the app, in its Singapore region), Neon (the database, in Singapore) and Cloudflare R2 (uploaded files). They handle the data on the operator's behalf.
