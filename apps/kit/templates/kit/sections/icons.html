Icons

Lucide icons from one sprite, used sparingly. A button carries an icon only to download, close, open the menu or sign out; tags, figures and navigation items never do. Checklists keep their met and not-met icons because they sit beside the words. Icons are decorative by default; give one a label when it is the only content of a control.
