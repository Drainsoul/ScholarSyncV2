Help and frequently asked questions

The answers are general and may be edited by whoever runs a copy.

About this site

Is this an official City of Taguig service?

No. ScholarSync is an independent portfolio project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of Taguig, and all sample people and data are fictional.

How do I try each role?

Run a copy on your computer with docker compose up. It includes fictional test accounts for a student, program staff and the scholarship office. The README lists them.

Where does my data go on a copy that runs on my computer?

Nowhere else. It stays in the database on your computer, and emails go to a local inbox, not to real people.

Who looks after the data on a hosted copy?

The operator of that copy. See the privacy policy.

For students

Who can apply?

Each program sets its own rules, such as residency, school and grades. Build your profile first, and ScholarSync shows the programs you may qualify for.

Which documents do I need?

Each program lists the documents it requires. You upload them with your application.

How do I follow my application?

Sign in and open your applications. The status shows where each one stands, and you get a notice when it changes.

How do I track my allowance?

If you are awarded a scholarship, your allowances are shown term by term, with their payment status.

Does applying guarantee an award?

No. The program decides, under its own rules.
